        From raw data to fluxes by scale

        1. In EddyPro, save the processed raw data: Advanced Settings, Output Files, Processed raw data, time series on “level 6 (after time lag compensation)”, all variables.
        2. Load those files here. They are put in time order from their names.
        3. Choose covariances and conditional samplings, for example w*co2 sampled by w*h2o for NEE, GPP and Reco.
